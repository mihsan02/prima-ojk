Metodologi
Pasal 50 (Risiko Pasar): simulasi penurunan harga volatile (ETH, BTC, SOL) −25%/−50%/−80% dan stablecoin (USDT, USDC) −3%/−8%/−13%. Threshold: ekuitas pasca-stress ≥ Rp 50.000.000.000 (Pasal 50(1)(o) POJK 27/2024). Basis historis: BTC −64% sepanjang 2022 (Chainalysis 2024), BTC −84% siklus 2017–2018 (CoinMarketCap), USDC depeg USD 0,87 saat kolaps SVB Maret 2023 (Reuters).

Pasal 91 (Risiko Siber): simulasi kehilangan AKD konsumen −23%/−50%/−100%. Threshold sama: ekuitas pasca-kehilangan ≥ Rp 50.000.000.000. Basis historis: GDAC April 2023 −23% (Chainalysis 2024), WazirX Juli 2024 −50% (Elliptic/Reuters), Mt Gox 2014 −100% AKD konsumen (public record).
